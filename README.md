# Investment-Portfolio-Questionnaire-Analysis
This is my project analysing data gathered from questionnaires about investment portfolios during the COVID-19 lockdown period. I have created multiple queries using MySQL and ported these queries into a PowerBI dashboard to showcase this data. 

Dataset Used:
<a href=https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/nitindatta/finance-data>Dataset</a>
Questions KPIs:
•	Q1. Gender ratio of investors
•	Q2. Age groups of investors
•	Q3. Most important investment method vs least important
•	Q4. Reasons and Risks for investing 
•	Q5. Sources based on their savings objectives 
•	Q6. Avenues by investment duration and how often they monitor their investments
•	Q7. Reasons why investors would choose equity as their avenue
•	Q8. Reasons why investors would choose Mutuals as their avenue
•	Q9. Reasons why investors would choose Government Bonds as their avenue
•	Q10. Reasons why investors would choose Fixed Deposits as their avenue

Project Insight

Majority of investors are aged 25+. In investors under 25 the number of investors are quite close with there being slightly more female investors. However, with those aged 25+ the higher number seems to be from men. 

Mutual funds is ranked the most important by the responders with ranked most important reasons being that it has better returns. This is usually due to the diversity of investments that come from a mutual fund and that it is also in the hands of a hedge fund manager, this creates a more risk-free outcome for mutual funds.

Financial Consultants seem to be the more popular source of information mainly because it is the most trusted source being the most present for those saving for healthcare and retirement.
Newspapers and magazines are the next popular option being the second most present source of information for those investing in healthcare and retirement. This one may appeal to the older responders of the questionnaire who are more likely to read magazines and newspapers instead of getting it from the internet or anywhere else.

Television is the least common source of information mainly used for retirement, healthcare and eduactation with low numbers for all savings objectives.

Investors mainly chose Equity, owning stocks and shares in companies due to its capital appreciation as the value of shares increase overtime based on a company’s performance. Buying stocks and shares is usually the more common and straightforward method of investing among most people. 

For Mutual funds, the most common reason is due to better returns, this goes hand-in-hand with fund- diversification as investing in multiple companies gives more opportunity for growth, alongside this mutual funds are usually handled by a professional hedge-fund manager which will be experienced and trustworthy to yield better returns.

The most common reason for Government Bonds is due to its assured returns, a more reliable and less risky investment as a return is garunteed if the bonds are held for the entire term.

The biggest reason for Fixed Deposits are used are due to its risk-free nature. There is usually no financial risk as long as the money isnt withdrawn too early.
